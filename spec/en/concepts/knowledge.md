# Knowledge

## Definition

Knowledge is any information intentionally retained by the user that may contribute to future understanding or decision-making.

Knowledge is owned by the user.

Enxame never owns knowledge.

## Responsibilities

- Organize knowledge.
- Catalog knowledge.
- Relate knowledge.
- Index knowledge.
- Retrieve knowledge.
- Summarize knowledge.
- Track its evolution over time.

## It is NOT responsible for

- Determining truth.
- Replacing human judgment.
- Discarding conflicting information.
- Deleting user knowledge without authorization.
- Modifying user knowledge without authorization.

## Relationships

- Knowledge belongs to one user.
- Knowledge may originate from multiple sources.
- Knowledge may reference other knowledge.
- Knowledge may become obsolete.
- Knowledge may conflict with other knowledge.
- Knowledge may evolve over time.

## Invariants

- Knowledge always belongs to the user.
- Knowledge always preserves provenance whenever possible.
- Knowledge is never silently modified.
- Conflicting knowledge is preserved.
- Historical knowledge is never confused with current knowledge.
- Enxame organizes knowledge.
- Humans interpret knowledge.

## Future Extensions

This concept may evolve through EIPs.

Changes to its invariants require architectural review.
